Never chains questions. Tapping Skip or an unmapped answer immediately renders the best-effort top-8 shortlist.

[Annotation 4.1] Strict Max-8 Item Ceiling

Template-generated strings ("Matches relational 'roommate' + visual 'Blue Bottle'") guarantee zero hallucination.
