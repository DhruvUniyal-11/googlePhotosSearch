10 Risks, Limitations & Strategic Mitigations

Bounding AI semantic intelligence with deterministic safeguards, strict UX limits, and privacy enclaves
Strict 1-Turn Cap Private On-Device Enclave
🧠 Algorithmic & Model
Decomposition Drift Med Severity
LLM misclassifies idioms or slang (e.g. "sick trip" mapped to medical illness instead of vacation).
Mitigation: Active category score normalization + deterministic templated match explanations (0 hallucinations).
People Graph Cold Start High Severity
User hasn't tagged relational roles (who is "roommate" vs "sister") in their library.
Mitigation: 1-tap chip tagging in photo detail + face co-occurrence inference + fallback to visual/place signals.
💬 Product & UX Guardrails
Clarification Fatigue High Risk
Google Photos is an instant utility, not a chatbot. Asking multiple questions induces user annoyance.
Mitigation: Hardcoded 1-turn maximum (never chains a 2nd question) + visible instant "Skip" button.
False-Match Anchoring Med Risk
Showing green "High Match" badges on wrong photos quickly erodes user trust in AI search.
Mitigation: 1.25x synergy multiplier requires co-occurrence of independent signals; scores < 0.40 strictly filtered.
⚙️ Technical & Scale
Inference Latency & Cost High Cost
Calling cloud LLM for every keystroke across 1B+ MAU is financially and latency prohibitive.
Mitigation: Two-tier architecture: lightweight distilled SLM on-device (Gecko 250M) + pre-computed local HNSW vectors.
Personal Document Privacy Critical
Parsing receipts, invoices, and family contacts triggers acute user privacy anxiety.
Mitigation: 100% on-device secure enclave processing; zero server image retention (proven in MVP browser sandbox).
🔬 Research & Non-Goals
Survey vs. Interview Gap Assumption
Target priority (#1 Relational, #2 Document) is survey-corrected (n=39) but pending qualitative interviews.
Mitigation: Explicitly acknowledged as active assumption; modular scoring weights dynamically retuned post-study.
Deliberate Scope Boundaries Scope
Excludes Stage 1 (expression is not broken) and Stage 3 (distributed index rewrite).
Focus: Targets Stage 2 (System Understanding, 54.5% of failures) where semantic intelligence has highest ROI.
Strategic Summary: Constraining intelligence to Stage 2 avoids conversational bloat while solving 54.5% of root-cause retrieval failures. Tested in MVP Sandbox: Strict 1-Turn Cap & Isolated Client Storage
